\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
Wireless contention is important when packet arrivals change over time.
Our exercise studies how queue occupancy affects activity in a single cell.
A fixed-traffic abstraction does not represent the queue-dependent activity considered here.
In this paper, we formulate a queue-aware fixed-point model and use a damped iterative solver.
The contributions of this paper are summarized as follows:
\begin{itemize}
\item We formulate a queue-aware fixed-point model connecting queue occupancy and contention probability.
\item We implement a damped iterative solver and compare its predictions with simulation for 20 stations at loads of 0.2, 0.4, and 0.6 packets per slot. The relative error is below 5\% in these supplied settings.
\end{itemize}
